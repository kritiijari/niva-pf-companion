EPFO employee services provide guidance for maintaining member information and using UAN-based online services. Employment and service information is relevant to claim processing, and incorrect or incomplete records may need correction through the appropriate EPFO and employer process. This local extract is a short, reviewed index of official EPFO employee guidance and is not a replacement for the official portal or documents.
