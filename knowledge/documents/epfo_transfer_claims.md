EPFO's transfer-claim guidance explains that a member can select previous PF accounts for transfer and retrieve details of previous employment accounts. The transfer process uses information from previous and current employment records, including member and account details. This local extract is a short, reviewed index of official EPFO transfer guidance and is not a replacement for the official portal or documents.
