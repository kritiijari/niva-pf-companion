EPFO guidance covers completion of KYC through the member portal and states that bank account details are part of the KYC process. EPFO also provides guidance for cases where member information does not match the records used for verification. This local extract is a short, reviewed index of official EPFO guidance and is not a replacement for the official portal or documents.
